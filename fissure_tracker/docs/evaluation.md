# Evaluation

Generated by `python tools/evaluate.py` on 24 Oct 2026 in 186 s.

**How:** almost everything here uses **synthetic photos with a known answer**: a rendered plaster wall with a
crack of known width, photographed by a virtual phone camera (focal length 0.72 × image width) from a known
distance and angle. This tests the geometry and the measurement honestly, but real photos add lens distortion,
uneven light and real wall texture. Real-photo results are listed separately below.

Uncertainty = ±1 pixel in the straight-on view (1 / px per mm). "px/mm" is how many pixels one millimetre
of wall covers in the photo: about 4 at 30 cm with a 1600 px-wide photo, about 10 for a close-up.

## 6. Failure cases

| case | what happened | notes |
|---|---|---|
| Straight pencil line (no crack) | measured as a 'crack' 0.61 mm | Limitation: colour/shape can't tell a drawn line from a crack. The owner is told what was measured and the marked-up photo shows it. |
| Round dark stain (24 mm) | ignored | Wide dark areas and their rims are blanked out before looking for cracks. |
| 1.0 mm crack running through that stain | found, 1.16 mm wide, 68 of ~100 mm long | The part inside the stain is not measured (limitation). |
| Faint 0.4 mm hairline (25 grey levels darker than the wall) | found, 0.59 mm (flagged too thin -> close-up) | Faint, thin cracks read too wide: the agent's close-up loop exists for this. |
| Photo taken 40° from the side | too_tilted | Agent asks for a straight-on retake. |
| Plain wall with no texture (alignment) | plain_wall | Alignment skipped, widths still compared; not blamed on the owner. |
| Close-up vs normal photo (alignment) | scale_too_different | Agent lines up with the latest photo of similar scale instead. |

## Limits of this evaluation

- Synthetic photos have no lens distortion and an assumed focal length; the tilt estimate depends on it.
- The synthetic wall texture is random grain; real walls have pores, paint and dirt.
- Real crack growth takes weeks: growth was simulated by drawing a wider crack.
- The decision thresholds (2 mm alert, 0.2 mm growth) are project settings, not engineering standards.
